The test counts differ from the ones 4D-1 recorded (168 unit, 142 browser), and nothing is missing:

- `ghost_unittests` runs from `out/release`, whose signing identity is `test`. That build adds `CrxVerifierTest.TestIdentityRejectsTheDevelopmentPublisher`, which a development build doesn't have.
- `ghost_browsertests` lists `NetworkDefaultsBrowserTest.PRE_GpcCanBeTurnedOff` as a test of its own, and the launcher's summary counts it as part of `GpcCanBeTurnedOff`.

## What went wrong, and what changed

1. **The start came 35 h 35 min after the issue.** This used up most of the margin: the work took 11 h, and the deadline was 17 h 57 min away when it ended. GitHub's notification for the issue went unnoticed. A release needs 11 to 16 h on this machine, so it must start by T₀ + 56 h at the latest, and by T₀ + 48 h to leave room for one failed stage. Nothing changed in the tooling. A release that has to be watched for needs a notification that reaches the maintainer, and that question goes to the maintainer.
2. **A wrong total in the issue.** The closing comment first gave 30 h 3 min from T₀ and 41 h 57 min to spare, a day off. It was corrected in place, and the figures above are the right ones. Compute totals from the timestamps, not by hand.

## What differed from a real release

- The test signing identity, and a draft: the name's trademark gate is open.
- No rollout: the update server isn't deployed (sub-project C).
